Privacy Policy

Deliverance Prayer (Android app, package org.jsrc.deliveranceprayer)
Effective date: 16 November 2026

Deliverance Prayer is a free Catholic prayer app developed by Lejin Babu Varghese with the blessing of the Director of Jesus Sangetha Bhavan Retreat Centre (JSRC), Pathanamthitta. This policy explains what information the app uses and how.

Summary

  • No account or sign-in is needed.
  • The app does not collect, store or share any personal information.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Information stored on your phone

To remember your choices, the app saves a few settings only on your device: your language, font size, dark mode choice, favourite prayers and their order, Manna card style, and the verses you save. This information never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

Photos

If you choose to use your own photo as a background for a Manna verse card, the app asks for permission to pick a photo. The photo is used only on your device to make the card. It is not uploaded or shared by the app.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app creates an image and text and hands them to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp). The app does not send them anywhere itself. What happens next is governed by the app you shared with.

Prayer content downloads

Prayers, Bible verses, images and other content are downloaded from GitHub Pages (lejinbv.github.io), a service run by GitHub, Inc. Like any website, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when the content is downloaded. The app does not send any personal information with these requests. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to other websites

Some buttons (for example Book Now) open websites or apps run by others, such as booking forms, WhatsApp or phone calls. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is suitable for everyone and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
